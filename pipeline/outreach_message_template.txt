Design notes (why this avoids reading as a scam):
- States who's writing and why in the first line — no anonymity.
- Leads with something free and already delivered (the video/report link),
  not a request. Nothing is asked of them before they see value.
- No urgency/scarcity language ("act now", "limited time", "spot reserved").
- No payment, personal info, or click-a-suspicious-link ask — the link
  goes to a page on GitHub they can inspect, not a redirect chain.
- The specific detail placeholder ({{DETAIL}}) should reference something
  real from the listing (bed count, a standout feature) so it doesn't read
  as a mass-blasted template, even though it's templated.
- WhatsApp is offered as a *next step after* they've seen value, not the
  first ask — reduces the "why would I move platforms for a stranger" friction.

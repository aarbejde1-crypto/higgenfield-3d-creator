# Production record — boiler room / pump and dump

**Style:** Editorial Motion Graphics (Explainer default), accent locked to **deep crimson**
**Voice:** pinned preset Cillian `d8ba9f14-…` · delivery "wry conversational explainer,
neutral accent, dry low timbre, lively pace"
**Format:** 9:16, 4 × 10s blocks = 40s, 2K, burned captions (bold / tiktok)

## Through-line, re-expressed for the style

manu-writer wrote the through-line against a neutral "near-black plane". Editorial Motion
Graphics locks a *warm cream paper stage*, so the through-line was re-expressed in the
style's own vocabulary rather than forced:

| Manuscript | Produced |
|---|---|
| token stack on a dark plane | stack of monochrome halftone paper cards on a cream collage stage |
| reserve block | solid deep crimson token slab — the style's single color-pop |
| filaments (phone lines) | hand-drawn crimson marker strokes fanning from one origin |
| the insiders | the style's "giant hand" device, which also keeps the video faceless |

## Voiceover rewrite

manu-writer budgets 27–31 words per block (2.9 wps). This pipeline requires **20–23 words**
against a measured 7.8–9.5s speech window, so every line was rewritten shorter while keeping
the three things that carry the SEO and the loop:

- "pump and dump" spoken in the hook (audio is transcribed and indexed)
- "illegal" spoken in the hook (compliance, out loud, not just in the description)
- "a stock worth nothing" reused verbatim in the payoff (drives replays)

Measured takes, after one retry on blocks 3 and 4:

| Block | Speech | Words | wps | Pauses | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8.13s | 23 | 2.83 | 0 | pass |
| 2 | 8.05s | 20 | 2.48 | 0 | pass |
| 3 | 8.69s | 20 | 2.30 | 0 | pass (retry — first take ran 10.05s) |
| 4 | 8.45s | 23 | 2.72 | 0 | pass (retry — first take ran 7.65s) |

Block 3's first take was overlong because of a colon and two commas, not word count: TTS
pauses ~0.7s at a period and ~0.5s at a comma. Rewriting it comma-light fixed it at the
same 20 words.

# How a room full of phones turned a worthless stock into a fortune

**Channel:** finance / investing education, faceless · **Duration:** 40s (4 blocks) ·
**Aspect:** 9:16 · **Pace target:** 2.9 wps → 27–31 words per block

**Through-line — THE TOKEN STACK.** A stack of flat identical tokens on a vast
near-black plane. A hard reserve block snaps out of its base (calm) → filaments fan
out and the stack climbs into a bright tower (escalating) → the reserve block slides
out and the tower stands on nothing (peak) → it collapses to a dull spread while the
reserve block sits far off, large and untouched (resolved).

The last frame is the whole thesis in one picture: the scattered dull pile on one
side, somebody else's fortune on the other. Textless throughout.

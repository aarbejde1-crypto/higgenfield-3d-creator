nothing more. This is the budget cut of the 4-block manuscript in
`runs/2026-09-17-why-a-stock-can-gap-down-40-before-the-market/`, restructured so the
story still completes: the build and turn are folded into the payoff block rather than
dropped, so the video is short but not truncated.

**Honest tradeoff:** 20s sits below the 30–45s band where completion rate and
recommendation rate peak together. It will likely complete at a high percentage and earn
less total watch time than a 40s cut of the same idea.

**Zero new images.** Style key and all asset plates reused from the boiler-room run, so
every credit went to the two blocks and the narration.

**Through-line — THE PRICE STACK.** A tall stack of monochrome halftone cards whose
HEIGHT is the price, plus two crimson marker lines. The empty cream band between the two
lines *is* the gap — the idea is the picture.

## Block 1 — 0:00–0:10 — HOOK

> Your stock closed at fifty and opens at thirty, with nobody trading a single share in
> between. Traders call that a gap down. **(23 words · measured 8.54s)**

- **SHOT 1 — 0.0–2.0s — FULL-SPREAD, wide, low:** the tall card stack SLAMS onto the plinth; a crimson marker line SNAPS across level with its top. *(impact · cover frame)*
- **SHOT 2 — 2.0–4.0s — ECU, high:** that line resting exactly on the top card, ink settling. Slow push-in.
- **SHOT 3 — 4.0–6.0s — MEDIUM, eye:** a second crimson line draws itself far below and holds over bare cream paper. Gentle drift.
- **SHOT 4 — 6.0–8.0s — WIDE, low:** both lines in frame, a tall band of bare paper between them. Whip down across the empty band.
- **SHOT 5 — 8.0–10.0s — CLOSE, low:** the stack's upper section TEARS AWAY in one beat, leaving a torn edge sitting on the lower line. Settles. *(impact)*

## Block 2 — 0:10–0:20 — PAYOFF

> Overnight the market is shut, so sell orders queue. The open is an auction at one price,
> and nobody traded the space between. **(23 words · measured 8.37s)**

- **SHOT 1 — 10.0–12.0s — CLOSE, eye:** a torn-paper shutter SLAMS down across the grid field, covering the stack. *(impact)*
- **SHOT 2 — 12.0–14.0s — WIDE, high:** cards pile into a waiting queue in front of it — three, then a dozen, then a crowd, staggered.
- **SHOT 3 — 14.0–16.0s — ECU, low:** the shutter vanishes in one frame; every queued card funnels toward one crimson seam. Slow push-in.
- **SHOT 4 — 16.0–18.0s — MEDIUM, eye:** the seam STAMPS into a single crimson line across the field — one clearing price. *(impact)*
- **SHOT 5 — 18.0–20.0s — WIDE, high:** the short stack under a tall empty band, upper line stranded high above, lower line on the torn edge. Settles.
